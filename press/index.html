Description

A dragon has carried off the princess, and something darker is pulling its strings. Paper Heroes is an isometric dungeon crawler in which you fold up a hero and fight your way down a hundred floors to bring her home.

Features

  • 100 floors across 10 biomes, from the Crypt to the Dragon's Keep
  • 7 playable races, each with its own 25-tier skill tree
  • 100+ enemies and 20 floor bosses, all animated paper dolls
  • A story rolled fresh each run, told in pixel-art cutscenes, with six possible overlords
  • Puzzle gates, traps, mimics, a forge for fusing loot up to mythic, and hobgoblin allies
  • Touch controls on mobile; keyboard, mouse or controller on PC
