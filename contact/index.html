Getting help with Paper Heroes

Many questions are answered in the FAQ, including restoring Remove Ads on a new phone and changing your ad consent.

If you're reporting a bug, it helps if you include:

  • your device (for example, "Pixel 8, Android 15" or "Windows 11 PC")
  • the game version, shown in the bottom-left corner of the screen
  • what you were doing when it happened, and the floor you were on
  • a screenshot, if you can

Privacy requests

Paper Heroes doesn't send us any personal information. See the Paper Heroes privacy policy for what the game stores and how Google's ads and purchases work. If you have a privacy question or request, email us at the address above.
