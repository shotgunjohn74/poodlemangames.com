Paper Heroes

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026. Updated for Google Play Games sign-in and cloud saves, and the game's links to our website and social media.

Who we are

Paper Heroes is a game made by Poodleman Games LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, who handles it, and the choices you have. It covers the Android app (package name com.poodlemangames.paperheroes), including the version on Google Play and the no-ads version, plus the desktop and iOS versions.

Summary

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you. There are no accounts, analytics or crash reports of our own.

  • Your saved games and settings stay on your device, unless you choose to sign in with Google Play Games. If you do, a copy of your saved characters is kept in your own Google account, so they follow you to a new phone. Google stores that copy, not us.
  • The Google Play version shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, such as your advertising ID, to serve and measure those ads.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details.
  • The no-ads Android version, the desktop version and the iOS version make no internet connections at all.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few things in its own private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us.

Saved characters and runs: the names you type, appearance, level, gear, gold and dungeon progressSo you can continue where you left off

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include this data in your backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google under your account settings, not by us.

Google Play Games sign-in and cloud saves (Google Play version)

Signing in is optional, and the game works fully without it. The Google Play version can sign you in to Google Play Games, a service of Google LLC. That happens automatically when your device is already signed in to Play Games, or when you tap Sign in with Google Play on the title screen or in Settings.

While you're signed in:

  • Your name: the game receives your Play Games display name, only to show "Signed in as" on screen. It isn't stored.
  • Your saved characters: a copy is kept in Play Games' Saved Games, in your Google account. That covers the names you typed, appearance, level, gear, gold and dungeon progress, plus the list of characters you deleted described above.
  • Syncing: the game updates that copy a few seconds after you save, and reads it back when you sign in. That's how your characters come back on a new phone.

The copy goes straight from your device to Google and is read back only by the game on your devices. We can't see it, and we never receive it.

Google handles your Play Games profile, sign-in and saved games under the Google Privacy Policy. Play Games may also record that you played Paper Heroes in your Play Games profile and activity. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its saved games, including this game's, in the Play Games settings on your device or at myaccount.google.com.

Links to our website and social media

The title screen and the pause menu's Follow Us page link to our website and to our YouTube, Facebook and Instagram pages. A tap opens the page in your web browser. The game itself sends nothing to those sites. Once you're there, the site's own privacy policy applies.

Advertising (Google Play version)

The Google Play version shows a short video ad before you save, load, start a new game or go down to the next floor. These ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. We do not receive any personal information from AdMob, only overall earnings reports.

To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, and on newer Android versions, signals from Android's Privacy Sandbox (ad topics and ad attribution)
  • your IP address, and a rough location worked out from it
  • device details, such as model, operating system version, language and network type
  • how you interact with the ads, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped

Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests (personalized ads). Google's use of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game first asks for your consent using Google's consent form. Your answer decides whether AdMob may show personalized ads, or only limited, non-personalized ones. No ads are requested until the form has been answered. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Options.

You can reset your advertising ID, or delete it to opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices, Settings → Google → Ads). Buying Remove Ads stops the game from loading ads at all.

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the whole payment, including your payment method and billing details, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms. We never see your card or bank details.

To confirm the purchase, Google Play gives the game a purchase record: the product bought, its status (completed or pending) and a purchase reference code. The game uses it only on your device to switch the ads off and to confirm the purchase back to Google Play. It keeps only a yes/no note that you own Remove Ads. The purchase is tied to your Google account, so reinstalling the game or moving to a new phone restores it.

Versions without ads

The no-ads Android version, the desktop version and the iOS version contain no advertising, purchase or Play Games sign-in code, and make no internet connections of their own. The Follow Us links open your web browser. The no-ads Android version doesn't even request internet access. In these versions, everything the game stores stays on your device, as described above. On desktop, downloadable content (DLC) is read from files on your own computer; the game doesn't download it or report it to anyone.

Children's privacy

Paper Heroes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child's information has been collected through the ads in the game, contact us and we'll help you reach Google to have it removed.

Sharing, retention and security

We don't sell, rent or share personal information, because we don't receive any. The only other company involved is Google: for ads (AdMob), for purchases (Google Play), and, if you sign in, for Play Games and its cloud saves. Google collects information directly, as described above, under its own policies.

What the game stores on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the game. It's kept in the game's private storage, which other apps can't read. Information Google collects for ads and purchases is kept according to the Google Privacy Policy. Your cloud saves stay in your Google account until you delete them through your Play Games settings. Deleting a character in the game also removes it from your cloud saves the next time the game syncs.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your game data: delete a save in the game's character screen, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it in Android settings. That removes everything the game stored on your device.
  • Cloud saves: they're optional, so just don't sign in to Google Play Games. To remove a copy already in your Google account, delete the game's saved data, or your Play Games profile, in your Play Games settings.
  • Limit ad tracking: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's ad settings (see Advertising above). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you can also change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Stop ads entirely: buy Remove Ads, or play the no-ads version.
  • Data held by Google: view, download or delete it, and manage ad personalization, at myaccount.google.com.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK) or the CCPA (California) may give you the right to access or delete your personal information. We hold none, but if you have a question or request, contact us and we'll help.

Changes and contact

If the game starts handling information differently, we'll update this policy, post the new version at the same address, and change its effective date. For anything major, we'll also mention it in the game's update notes.
