Paper Heroes

Paper Heroes 1.28 is heading to Google Play

Version 1.28 is the first Google Play release. What's in it:

  • 100 floors across 10 biomes, from the Crypt to the Dragon's Keep.
  • 7 playable races, each with its own 25-tier skill tree and abilities.
  • Over 100 enemies and 20 floor bosses, plus six overlords waiting on floor 100.
  • A one-time Remove Ads purchase that turns off ads for good, and carries over to a new phone.
  • Ad consent for players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, with Privacy Options in Settings.

We'll post here as soon as it's live.
