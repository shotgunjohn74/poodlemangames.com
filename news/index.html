Paper Heroes 23 November 2026

Paper Heroes is coming to iPhone and iPad

We're bringing Paper Heroes to iOS. It's the same game as on Android and Windows, with the same touch controls and no ads, and it adds:

  • Game Center achievements: all 25 achievements from the Android version.
  • Game Center leaderboards: the fastest Daily Dungeon of the day, and the deepest floor reached in Endless Depths.
  • iCloud saves: your heroes follow your Apple account to a new iPhone or iPad, with no sign-in in the game.

It will need iOS 14 or later. We'll post the App Store link here when it's out.

Paper Heroes 23 November 2026

Paper Heroes 1.41

An Android update, coming to Google Play:

  • Allies: every ally now gets a panel, and you can swipe through them when there are more than fit. Tap one to open its card, then swipe the card left or right to see the next ally.
  • The MAP button shows and hides the minimap again.
  • The phone's Back button now backs out of menus instead of doing nothing.
  • Fixed text overlapping on the Collections tabs, the inventory's equipped slots and the Daily Dungeon's messages.
  • Phones with screens under 720 pixels tall now get a notice instead of a game that doesn't fit.

Get it on Google Play

Paper Heroes 20 November 2026

Paper Heroes 1.40

Another small update, coming to Google Play:

  • Collections: a boss you've beaten in the Daily Dungeon now shows its picture and name on the Bosses page, not just its slain count. Any that were missing fill in by themselves.
  • Paper Heroes can now be installed on more devices, including Chromebooks and tablets played with a keyboard or controller.

Get it on Google Play

Paper Heroes 18 November 2026

Paper Heroes 1.39

A small update with fixes, coming to Google Play:

  • Daily Dungeon: in a no-potions week, no potions turn up at all. Drops that would have been potions are gold instead, and the merchant doesn't sell any. The POT button is greyed out, and the potion counter reads NO POTIONS THIS WEEK.
  • Fixed a search sparkle floating over empty floor where a weapon rack or armour stand had been cleared out of the way.
  • Fixed your hero being drawn on top of a puzzle block while pushing it from behind.

Get it on Google Play

Paper Heroes

Paper Heroes 1.28 is heading to Google Play

Version 1.28 is the first Google Play release. What's in it:

  • 100 floors across 10 biomes, from the Crypt to the Dragon's Keep.
  • 7 playable races, each with its own 25-tier skill tree and abilities.
  • Over 100 enemies and 20 floor bosses, plus six overlords waiting on floor 100.
  • A one-time Remove Ads purchase that turns off ads for good, and carries over to a new phone.
  • Ad consent for players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, with Privacy Options in Settings.

We'll post here as soon as it's live.
